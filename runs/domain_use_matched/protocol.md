# Objective task selection with calibrated projection interventions

Development follow-up, frozen before outputs on 2026-09-12. This tests a narrow behavioral proxy, not open-ended harmfulness or human-validated relevance.

Twenty-four new scenarios belong to eight arithmetic operation families. Each has paired finance/non-finance tasks, competing information present/absent, and two answer-label permutations: 192 prompts. Three generation seeds yield 576 answers per condition, ten conditions, 5,760 answers. Choice sets are fixed across task relevance and distractor presence within each scenario/permutation. Correct/distractor answers are known from construction. Only an entire A/B/C/D response (optionally followed by a period) is valid; other output and truncation count as failure. No LLM judge determines these primary labels. Scenario labels do not make the repeated operation templates independent: primary uncertainty resamples the eight operation families; 24-scenario intervals are secondary sensitivity analyses.

Conditions use the previous fixed vectors: historical domain, benign topic, within-finance harmfulness, five randoms, plus finance baseline and aligned base. Intervene at layers 12,16,20,24,28 using h' = h - alpha * projection_u(h). Alpha=1 is ordinary ablation; alpha>1 reverses part of the projected component and is NOT ordinary ablation. The experiment calibrates projection strength, not an equal-rank ablation comparison.

Choose one alpha per direction using 16 fixed benign prompt/reference-answer pairs, matching mean KL from the unmodified finance model across answer-token predictive distributions. The target is the historical direction at alpha=1. Search the frozen grid for the first crossing, then seven bisections; keep the closest evaluated dose. Preserve all calibration measurements. A condition outside 15% of target is flagged unmatched. Eight separate benign references assess whether the match generalizes; these are not used to select doses. Baseline and aligned base are reference conditions, not calibrated perturbations. Compare activation energy as an additional diagnostic; matched KL does not imply matched energy or matched effects on generated text.

Primary screen for the historical direction: reduce selection of the finance distractor on non-finance tasks with the distractor present (paired operation-family 95% interval for change below zero), and retain correctness on finance-required tasks with distractor present (lower bound greater than -5 pp). Report target correctness, distractor-choice rate, and invalid-format rate over ALL answers. Also report presence-versus-absence interaction, finance versus non-finance effects, calibration validation, all controls, and both family/scenario uncertainty. Record first-token choice probabilities and total choice-letter probability mass as secondary diagnostics; probabilities conditioned on four letters do not substitute for free-generation performance.

The rubric inconsistencies from the first study will be exported as blinded human-annotation material. No human labels are available at launch. Do not relabel old outcomes or call this follow-up a human-validated or confirmatory generalization study. No post-outcome task selection, dose tuning, or causal-localization decision may be presented as prespecified. If the screen fails, report the negative or inconclusive result and stop before causal patching.
