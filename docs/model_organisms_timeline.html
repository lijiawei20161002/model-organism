How to interpret this map. This is a selected corpus of 27 works, not a census or a lab ranking. Rows are editorial groupings, not separate research lineages. Solid arrows mean a later work explicitly extends, replicates or examines the earlier work; each relationship has a linked source in the details. Dashed connections mean shared authorship only. Color indicates one team for orientation; the written label records collaborations.

Dates use the first public release month, not conference acceptance dates. Author lists follow the linked versions available on 2026.10.03; author counts refer only to this corpus. Team labels are abbreviated and can include programs such as MATS. Where an institutional affiliation was not established, the researchers are named instead. Research posts and papers/preprints are labeled separately. Empty cells mean no work was selected for this map.
